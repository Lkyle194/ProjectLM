# Summary of ProjectLM — LLMMORPG Anti-Meta

## Game Concept Analysis

**ProjectLM** (by Laurent Ngea) is a design concept for an **LLM-powered MMORPG** with a radical anti-meta philosophy. The core idea is to create a living MMO where no optimized build is reproducible — skills, passives, and items are dynamically assigned by a **behavioral observation AI** that studies each player's actual playstyle in real time. The explicit goal is to kill the meta economy, render online guides useless, and reward authentic play.

## Core Mechanics

The central mechanic is an AI that tracks players across **invisible behavioral axes** (e.g., "protector", "coward", "opportunist", "heroic", "contemplative"). Every action — combat, fleeing, healing, exploring, emoting, dialogue — feeds XP into hidden bars linked to potential skills. When a bar reaches 100%, the skill enters a "waiting for manifestation" state. During a contextually appropriate event (a critical fight, a strong social moment, a special exploration find), the skill triggers automatically as an ultimate ability (e.g., summoning a giant skeleton mid-battle). After this first dramatic manifestation, the skill enters the player's kit in a normal, toned-down version.

**Item generation** follows the same philosophy: rare loot is dynamically created by the AI based on recent player behavior and emotional context. A solo player who often flees group fights might loot a "Traitor's Dagger" that deals extra damage while retreating but causes self-bleeding when attacking in a group. A healer whose team takes heavy losses might loot a "Last Bastion Wand" that charges on healing allies and explodes in a zone heal on ally KO.

**Skill evolution** is organic — skills can merge, transform, or branch into variants as the player's behavior shifts. A skeleton summoner who fights alongside their minion in melee might see the skill evolve into "Bone Armor" (passive damage reduction), eventually transforming the player's gameplay from summoner to death knight. Variants can combine with each other or completely change a skill's gameplay.

## Anti-Meta Systems

The AI actively detects artificial or repetitive behavior (spamming incoherent actions to farm a specific skill) and counteracts it by reducing earned XP, offering a different but more appropriate skill, or simply ignoring forced actions. The design intent is that skills are born from playstyle, loot from decisions, and evolution from unconscious habits — the player discovers themselves through the game rather than optimizing an external build. The README also includes a detailed standardized skill template for proposing new skills, quantifying behavioral XP sources, counter-conditions, ultimate manifestation contexts, and possible transformation variants.
